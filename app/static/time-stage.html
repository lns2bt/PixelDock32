Advanced Settings: Schedule, Scenes, Events & Location

Schedule

0 deactivates the respective trigger. Regular playlist entries are counted, not shuffle entries or “now” plays. Both triggers can be combined. The time limit is soft: running GIF loops and scrolling texts are allowed to end. Old events are discarded after tolerance.

Own scene profiles

Up to eight custom profiles. Includes scenes, weights, favorites, selection, movement and color - not the sleep schedule. Then save changes.

Clock views

The selection and order apply to your own profile. Weights work in random selection; Stars mark favorites.

Events

Each category has its own selection and duration: magical moments 6-60 seconds, celestial ceremonies 30-60 seconds. To turn off, deactivate the category; at least one scene remains selected.

Location

The default is the weather location. Custom coordinates are optional; if you have your own source, both are required.

No unsaved changes.

Browser preview

Both previews also use unsaved settings. Date, time and pace are only simulated in the browser. LED preview immediately shows the scene at the current time, then the playlist continues. It remains locked in sleep mode.
